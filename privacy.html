Map Explains Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Map Explains is a small publishing tool run by one person for the TikTok account @the_map_explains. It uploads the account owner's own short geography videos to that same account.

What we access

When the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and the basic profile of that one account (name and avatar). It uses the Content Posting API only to send the owner's own videos and captions to that account.

What we store

Only the authorization token, kept in a private, access-restricted location, and a log of which videos were sent. We do not collect data about viewers or any other TikTok users.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. After removal the stored token is deleted on request.

Contact

Message the TikTok account @the_map_explains.
